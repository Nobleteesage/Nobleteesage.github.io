Ogoniland land cover change, 1990 to 2023

Rivers State, Nigeria4.7° N, 7.3° ELandsat 5, 7 and 8Google Earth Engine, Python

A multi-decade remote sensing study of Ogoniland's four local government areas, tracking land cover before and after the 2011 UNEP Environmental Assessment of Ogoniland. Cloud-masked dry-season Landsat composites for three epochs were classified with NDVI and MNDWI thresholds, checked against K-Means clusters, then compared pixel by pixel and summarised per LGA.

Flood-disrupted hospital access, Yenagoa, 2022

Bayelsa State, Nigeria4.9° N, 6.3° EUNOSAT VIIRS flood extent, OpenStreetMapQGIS, QNEAT3, Python

A network accessibility analysis of how the October 2022 floods changed travel time to the nearest hospital in Yenagoa. Satellite-detected flood extent was intersected with the road network, travel speeds were assigned by road class, and iso-area analysis was run twice, once on the intact network and once with flooded roads removed.

M.Sc. thesis: reliability analysis of erasure-coded data centers

Southern Federal UniversitySupervisor: Prof. N.S. MogilevskayaPython 3.13, NumPy, SciPyDiscrete-event simulation

The SimEDC simulator for erasure-coded data centers no longer ran on current Python, and the failure dataset behind its published results was no longer available. I restored the simulator on Python 3.13, built a synthetic failure trace framework to replace the missing data, and used both to reproduce the original trace-driven reliability analysis. The first phase is complete and approved by my supervisor.

Solving large sparse linear systems with CG and PCG

Southern Federal University, 2026Numerical Methods of Linear Algebra for Sparse MatricesMATLABProject lead; slides by Ovolabani Ayobami

I led this joint project. I implemented the conjugate gradient method and its preconditioned form with an incomplete Cholesky preconditioner from scratch in MATLAB, then designed and ran the experiments on symmetric positive definite systems, including the gr30×30 matrix from the NIST Matrix Market (n = 900). Both solvers match MATLAB's built-in pcg() in every test. The same solvers sit at the core of large least-squares problems such as photogrammetric bundle adjustment.

7

10−8

Conditional expectation and Bayes prediction

Southern Federal University, 2026Machine Learning: Mathematical BasisPython, Jupyter

For Y = X + Z, with X uniform on [−1, 1] and Z exponential with rate 1, I derived the conditional density, mean and median of Y given X, the Bayes predictors under squared and absolute loss, and the risk minimizers within the family fa(x) = x + a. Because that family has the same slope as the true predictors, minimizing within it recovers the Bayes predictor exactly. A simulation with 5,000 samples confirms both results.

1.011
Empirical minimizer under squared loss, against the theoretical a = 1
0.706
Empirical minimizer under absolute loss, against the theoretical a = ln 2 ≈ 0.693
